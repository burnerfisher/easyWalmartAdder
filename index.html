Casserole

Taco Pie

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 96/4 lean ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 can (14 oz) tomato sauce
  • 1 package (10 oz) corn, drained
  • 6 tortillas
  • 1 cup Kraft fat-free shredded cheese

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Brown meat with onion and chili powder.
  3. Add tomato sauce and corn; simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Spoon 1 cup of mixture into a casserole dish. Cover with 3 tortillas, 2 cups of mixture, and ½ cup cheese.
  5. Top with remaining tortillas, then remaining meat mixture.
  6. Cover with foil and bake 25 minutes.
  7. Top with remaining cheese and bake 5 more minutes.

Casserole

Felicia's Cheesy Chicken Broccoli Rice Casserole

Ingredients

  • 2 cups shredded chicken (or Tyson black pepper chicken)
  • 1 bag frozen cauliflower rice
  • 1 bag frozen broccoli
  • ¼ cup light mayo
  • 1 can 98% fat-free cream of chicken soup
  • 1¼ cups fat-free cheddar
  • ¾ cup cornflakes

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Microwave or thaw the cauliflower rice and broccoli.
  3. Combine everything except the cornflakes and pour into a 9×13 dish.
  4. Crush the cornflakes and sprinkle on top.
  5. Bake 20–30 minutes.
Pasta

Chicken Stroganoff

Ingredients

  • 1 thin-sliced chicken breast (or Tyson black pepper chicken)
  • ⅓ cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • 64 g brown rice pasta
  • Onion
  • Mushroom
  • Spinach
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder

Directions

  1. Sauté all chopped veggies until tender.
  2. Meanwhile, boil water and cook pasta until tender.
  3. Dice, season, and cook the chicken breast.
  4. Mix chicken and veggies, then add drained pasta.
  5. Add cottage cheese and stir until melted.
Side

Cheesy Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 1 medium onion
  • 1 bag frozen cubed hash browns
  • 2 cans cream of chicken soup
  • 2 cups sour cream
  • 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
  • Salt and pepper
  • ½ cup butter, melted
  • 2 cups cornflakes

Directions

  1. Sauté onions.
  2. In a mixing bowl, combine cooked onions, hash browns, cream of chicken, sour cream, cheese, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread in a 9×13 baking dish.
  4. Coat cornflakes in melted butter and spread on top.
  5. Bake at 350°F for 50–55 minutes.
Side

Mac and Cheese

Ingredients

  • 1 block white cheddar
  • 1 block Colby Jack
  • 1 block sharp cheddar
  • 1 lb elbow macaroni
  • 12 oz evaporated milk
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream
  • Cajun seasoning, onion powder, garlic powder, white pepper
  • ½ stick butter

Directions

  1. Shred the cheese.
  2. Boil noodles, drain, and add to a baking dish.
  3. Slice butter and mix into the noodles.
  4. Combine heavy cream, evaporated milk, spices, and cheese (reserve a little for the top). Whisk until the cheese melts.
  5. Pour mixture over noodles and top with the reserved cheese.
  6. Broil until the cheese is browned.
